Click to place polygon points. Click near the first point (or hit "Close path") to finish. Once closed, drag any point to move it, double-click a point to remove it (min 3 points), or turn on "Add point mode" and click an edge to insert a new point there. Scroll to zoom, space-drag or middle-drag to pan. Zoom in for fine work — points are stored at source resolution, so accuracy is limited only by how far in you go.
